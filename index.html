Learn Model Evaluation with Phoebe

Two tracks into metrics that actually pay

Every metric is an opinion about which mistakes matter - and most teams inherit the opinion without noticing. This course covers both metric families in one arc: the classic canon (precision, recall, F1, ROC, calibration, cost matrices) and the LLM-era system (groundedness, hallucination rates, LLM-as-judge, latency and cost per query). A leader track teaches you to read any model report without being fooled. A practitioner track evaluates Lumen's real conversion model end to end - and a live evaluator runs in your browser: drag a real threshold on 2,000 scored sessions, press "Optimize F1" and watch it cost $704 more than the money-optimal point, then calibrate an LLM judge yourself and catch its verbosity bias red-handed.
